## Approach 1: "Golden Hour Americana" — Warm Editorial Folk

**Design Movement**: Editorial Folk / Americana — inspired by artisan music magazines and high-end winery branding

**Core Principles**:
- Warmth-first: every element evokes the golden hour feeling of an outdoor winery performance
- Generous whitespace with asymmetric editorial layouts — not centered grids
- Photography as the primary design element, text as supporting voice
- Tactile texture: subtle grain overlays, parchment-like backgrounds

**Color Philosophy**:
- Deep Espresso `#2C1810` — anchors the brand, evokes aged oak barrels
- Warm Amber `#C8860A` — primary accent, golden hour sunlight
- Cream `#F5EDD6` — background warmth, not stark white
- Sage Green `#5C7A5A` — vineyard foliage accent
- Soft Terracotta `#B85C38` — warmth, California earth

**Layout Paradigm**:
- Full-bleed hero with diagonal text overlay
- Staggered two-column sections alternating image/text
- Horizontal scrolling venue cards
- Sticky navigation with transparent-to-solid scroll behavior

**Signature Elements**:
- Musical staff / soundwave decorative dividers
- Handwritten-style script font for section labels
- Warm vignette overlays on all photography

**Interaction Philosophy**:
- Parallax scrolling on hero and venue images
- Hover reveals on venue cards (name + location slides up)
- Smooth section entrance animations (fade + slide from bottom)

**Animation**:
- Hero: subtle parallax drift on scroll
- Cards: staggered entrance with 100ms delays
- CTA button: gentle pulse on idle

**Typography System**:
- Display: Playfair Display (serif, editorial weight)
- Body: Lato (clean, readable)
- Accent labels: Dancing Script (handwritten warmth)

## Selected Approach: **Approach 1 — "Golden Hour Americana"**

This editorial folk aesthetic perfectly captures the warmth and intimacy of R & R Acoustic Duo's performances at wineries, wine bars, and community venues. The warm amber and espresso palette evokes the golden hour light of outdoor winery events, while the asymmetric editorial layout makes the site feel crafted and premium — exactly what venue owners and event coordinators need to see to feel confident booking the duo.
